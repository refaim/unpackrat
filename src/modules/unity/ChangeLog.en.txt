Version 1.0.1
-------------

 * Removed duplicate images from the resource list, including images listed in both {Sprite} and {Texture2D} folders.

Version 1.0.0
-------------

 * Opens Unity serialized files and asset bundles.
 * Extracts Texture2D and Sprite resources as PNG.
 * Extracts TextAsset resources as their original raw bytes.
 * Extracts AudioClip resources as WAV or Ogg, with legacy raw and FSB fallbacks.
 * Extracts font resources as TTF, OTF, or TTC.
 * Extracts VideoClip resources as raw containers and MovieTexture resources as OGV.
 * Exports Mesh resources as GLB geometry.
 * Exports requested resources on demand and keeps original resource paths when available, with {Type} fallbacks otherwise.
